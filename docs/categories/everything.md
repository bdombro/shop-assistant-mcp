# Everything

Marketplaces, classifieds and general retailers. Generic checks only. For a product type with its own category guide, read that too.

## Always check

- Seller: who sells and who ships. Marketplace listings differ from the platform's own stock
- Condition: new, used, refurbished, open-box, as the listing words it
- Location and shipping country. Customs or VAT may apply outside the EU. Convert other currencies and say you did
- Total cost: price + delivery + required extras
- Returns and buyer protection. Classifieds usually have neither
- Match the exact model, size, pack or revision before comparing prices. Same ASIN, SKU or EAN is the same product
- Rating: low counts are weak evidence. Seller feedback matters more than item stars on marketplaces

## Classifieds

- Used by default. Capture price, location (for distance to pickup), and ad age
- Old ads are often sold. Mark them
- Messaging sellers is not allowed. Give the user the link via `open`
- Scam signs to flag: price far below market, off-platform payment, no location
