# Home Improvement

Power tools, hand tools, fixings, timber, building supplies. Check these before you rank.

## Power tools

- Battery platform: same brand and voltage as the user's tools? Einhell 18V "Power X-Change" packs are shared across the range. Say so when a bare tool needs a pack the user lacks
- Bare tool vs kit: bare has no battery or charger. Price a kit against bare + pack + charger
- Brushless vs brushed, and the stated torque or power: copy the wording, don't compare across brands without a unit
- Included case, bits, guides
- Cordless vs corded: voltage claims (e.g. "20V max" = 18V nominal) are marketing

## Consumables and fixings

- Quantity: price per piece, not per box
- Size, thread, head type, length, material or coating (zinc, stainless, exterior)
- Compatible bits or drivers
- Blades, discs and bits must fit the tool's size and arbor

### Fastener vocabulary

- Head types are distinct, never substitutes: countersunk (flat, sits flush), double-countersunk (wood screws), pan (flat-topped dome), round/button (domed), hex. A "pan head" request isn't met by countersunk
- Size order is diameter × length in mm: "5x80" = 5 mm thread, 80 mm long. Shops write it "5 x 80", "5.0x80mm" or "5 x 80mm": search in the shop's wording
- Wood and chipboard screws (pointed, coarse thread) and machine screws (M sizes, need a nut or tapped hole) aren't interchangeable. M5 is a 5 mm machine thread
- Imperial gauges: No.8 ≈ 4.0 mm, No.10 ≈ 4.8 mm (sold as 5.0 mm metric wood screws), No.12 ≈ 5.5 mm
- Washers for M5 (5.3 mm hole): DIN 125 form A 10 mm outer, DIN 433 small 9 mm, DIN 9021 large (penny) 15 mm. Other sizes scale; read the stated outer diameter
- Drive: Pozi (PZ), Phillips (PH), Torx (TX/T), hex socket. PZ and PH bits aren't interchangeable

## Timber and sheet

- Nominal vs finished size (e.g. 2x4 sold as 38x89 mm)
- Length, grade, treatment, moisture (KD, PAR vs sawn), species
- Price per length vs per metre; delivery for long items, or yard pickup
- Sheet goods: thickness, grade, face finish

## Trade and retail

- Trade pricing may show ex-VAT or need a login. Record which prices include VAT
- Trade-counter stock and click-and-collect stock are per store. Name the store
- Delivery cutoffs, thresholds and fees change the total

## Report notes

- Distance to pickup per store, from the user's locations
- Returns and warranty on tools differ (often 2-3 years registered)
