# Electronics

Networking, computers, TVs, phones, appliances. Specs are marketing until verified.

## Always check

- Hardware revision: the same model name can ship as v1, v2 and v3 with different chips and firmware support. Find the revision on the listing or box. Mercusys routers are the known case
- Real specs vs marketing: speed class numbers (BE3600, AX1800) are summed band speeds. BE3600 is dual-band. Tri-band with a 6 GHz band is different from dual 5 GHz. Read the band list, not the class
- Standard and feature: Wi-Fi 7 vs 6E, HDMI 2.1 ports, USB4 vs USB-C-shaped USB 2.0, panel type
- Plug and region: UK or EU plug, 230 V, and EU or IE warranty. Grey imports and international sellers may lack them
- Warranty: length, who honours it, return window
- Firmware and support: update history, end of support, vendor app requirements
- Model number or EAN: use it to match the same product across sites. Rebrands share one

## Prices

- Per store vs central stock and pricing: some retailers show store-specific values. Say which
- Bundle deals: check what is included and what's required (cables, mounts)
- Open-box, refurbished, grade: record `condition` verbatim

## Reviews

- Read the critical ones for failure modes: reboots, drops, overheating, dead within a year
- Count themes, don't average stars
- Reviews for a different hardware revision don't count
